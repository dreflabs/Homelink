# 11. VALUE PROPOSITION
**HomeLink 2.0 Enterprise Documentation**

## 1. Title
HomeLink 2.0 Core Value Proposition Specification

## 2. Purpose
To distinctly articulate why a user (Buyer or Owner) should choose HomeLink 2.0 over established incumbents in the Indonesian PropTech market.

## 3. Scope
Covers the unique differentiators for both sides of the marketplace (Buyers and Owners).

## 4. Audience
- **Marketing & Copywriting Teams:** To ensure all external communications reflect the core value.
- **Designers:** To visually emphasize these propositions on the homepage.

## 5. Dependencies
- Derives directly from `01_VISION_AND_MISSION.md` and `02_BUSINESS_REQUIREMENT_DOCUMENT_BRD.md`.

## 6. Definitions
- **Value Proposition:** A promise of value to be delivered, communicated, and acknowledged.
- **Asymmetry of Information:** A market condition where one party (usually the broker/seller) has more information than the buyer.

## 8. Requirements

### 8.1. The "Why HomeLink?" Matrix

| Dimensi | Platform Properti Tradisional (Incumbents) | HomeLink 2.0 |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Integritas Listing** | Bebas untuk siapa saja mengunggah. Akibatnya: $\ge 40\%$ *Ghost Listing* dan data duplikat. | **100% Terverifikasi FIsik & Hukum.** Hanya properti hasil audit surveyor internal yang tayang. |
| **Desain & Estetika (UX)** | Padat, penuh iklan banner dari bank/developer, navigasi rumit. | **Apple-Level Premium Aesthetic.** Bersih, *whitespace* luas, tanpa iklan pihak ketiga sama sekali. |
| **Teknologi Pencarian** | Filter statis yang kaku (Dropdown lokasi, Slider harga). | **AI Semantic Search.** Pencarian bahasa alami layaknya berbicara dengan agen cerdas. |
| **Transparansi Transaksi** | Asimetri informasi. Harga sering kali bisa dinaikkan sepihak oleh perantara gelap. | **Transparan & Terlacak.** Harga divalidasi, laporan inspeksi legal dapat diunduh bebas oleh pengguna terdaftar. |

### 8.2. Core Copywriting Pillars
1. **Kejujuran Mutlak:** *"Apa yang Anda lihat di layar, persis sama dengan apa yang Anda dapatkan di lokasi."*
2. **Ketenangan Bernavigasi:** *"Cari rumah masa depan Anda tanpa diganggu pop-up iklan."*
3. **Penyelamat Waktu:** *"Jadwalkan survei dalam 3 klik. Tidak perlu menunggu balasan chat agen berjam-jam."*

## 9. Implementation
- The Homepage Hero Section MUST prominently display the core value proposition: "Properti Terverifikasi 100%. Bebas Ghost Listing." above the fold.
- The UI MUST NEVER incorporate ad-spaces (banner slots) that dilute this premium value proposition.

- [x] Value proposition is clearly differentiated from market competitors.
- [x] The language used is simple, authoritative, and professional.
