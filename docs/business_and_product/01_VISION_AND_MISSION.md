# 01. VISION & MISSION SPECIFICATION
**HomeLink 2.0 Enterprise Documentation**

## 1. Title
HomeLink 2.0 Strategic Vision and Mission Statement

## 2. Purpose
The purpose of this document is to define the fundamental North Star for HomeLink 2.0. It aligns all stakeholders, engineering teams, product managers, and design directors around a unified, unchangeable long-term goal.

## 3. Scope
This document covers:
- The overarching 5-year strategic vision.
- The core mission pillars that dictate daily operational and product decisions.
- The brand positioning within the Indonesian PropTech industry.

## 4. Audience
- **C-Level Executives:** For strategic alignment and investor relations.
- **Product & Design Teams:** To ensure features and UX reflect the premium standard.
- **Engineering Teams:** To understand the uncompromising standards for performance and security.
- **External Partners & Investors:** For evaluating business trajectory.

## 5. Dependencies
- None. This is a foundational root document (SSoT).

## 6. Definitions
- **PropTech:** Property Technology.
- **SSoT:** Single Source of Truth.
- **Ghost Listing:** Fraudulent, duplicated, or sold property listings that remain active on platforms to manipulate buyers.

## 7. Architecture
N/A (Business strategic level)

## 8. Requirements
### 8.1. Strategic Vision
Menjadi **"The Apple of the Indonesian Property Industry"** — platform PropTech terdepan di Indonesia yang menetapkan standar emas baru dalam transparansi, estetika, dan kecepatan transaksi properti dengan memadukan teknologi kecerdasan buatan (AI) dan verifikasi fisik 100% independen.

### 8.2. Mission Pillars
1. **Zero Ghost Listing Guarantee:** Menjamin bahwa setiap properti yang terdaftar di HomeLink 2.0 telah melalui inspeksi fisik dan hukum yang ketat oleh Surveyor resmi. Tidak ada kompromi pada integritas data.
2. **Apple-Level Premium Experience:** Menghadirkan antarmuka digital yang tenang, intuitif, sangat cepat (sub-second performance), dan sepenuhnya bebas iklan pihak ketiga yang mengganggu perjalanan pengguna.
3. **Pioneering Technology Leadership:** Mengadopsi teknologi terdepan (AI Semantic Search, LLM RAG, Next.js 16) untuk memberikan rekomendasi properti yang akurat bagaikan agen manusia.
4. **Empowering the Complete Ecosystem:** Menciptakan keseimbangan *win-win* bagi Pembeli (rasa aman), Pemilik (penjualan cepat), Agen (lead berkualitas), dan Surveyor (efisiensi kerja).

## 9. Implementation
- The Vision and Mission must be embedded in the onboarding process of every new HomeLink employee.
- The UX/UI teams must use Pillar 2 as the primary heuristic for usability testing.
