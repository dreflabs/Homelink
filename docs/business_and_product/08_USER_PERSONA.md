# 08. USER PERSONA
**HomeLink 2.0 Enterprise Documentation**

## 1. Title
HomeLink 2.0 User Persona Specifications

## 2. Purpose
To humanize the target audience of HomeLink 2.0 by creating detailed archetypes. This aligns the Product, Design, and Marketing teams on exactly who they are building the platform for.

## 3. Scope
Covers the two primary user archetypes: High-Intent Buyer and Secondary Market Owner.

## 4. Audience
- **UX/UI Designers:** To tailor the interface and copy to match the persona's reading level and emotional state.
- **Marketing Team:** To craft targeted acquisition campaigns.

## 5. Dependencies
- Directly influences the `09_USER_JOURNEY.md` and `10_CUSTOMER_JOURNEY_MAP.md`.

## 6. Definitions
- **High-Intent Buyer:** A user who is actively looking to purchase a property within the next 3-6 months and has secured funding (cash or KPR pre-approval).

## 8. Requirements

### 8.1. Persona 1: Arya Pratama (The High-Intent Buyer)
- **Demographics:** Male, 32 years old, Tech Manager, Married (1 toddler), Resides in Jakarta, Household Income $> Rp 40M/month$.
- **Goals:** Finding a permanent family home in the BSD/Tangerang Selatan area under Rp 2.5 Billion. Needs a fast commute to a train station.
- **Frustrations (Pain Points):** 
  - Exhausted from calling agents only to hear "Oh, that house is sold, but I have another one that is more expensive." (Bait and switch tactics).
  - Anxious about legal disputes regarding land certificates.
  - Hates cluttered websites with blinking ads.
- **Technical Literacy:** Extremely high. Uses Apple ecosystem products. Expects apps to be incredibly fast and well-designed.
- **HomeLink Solution Match:** The *100% Verified Only* badge gives him peace of mind, and the *Semantic AI Search* saves him hours of filtering.

### 8.2. Persona 2: Maya Indriani (The Cautious Owner)
- **Demographics:** Female, 45 years old, Small Business Owner, Resides in South Jakarta.
- **Goals:** Wants to sell her secondary home in South Jakarta at fair market value without being scammed by rogue brokers.
- **Frustrations (Pain Points):**
  - Found pictures of her house posted on 5 different property sites with 5 completely different prices by agents she never hired.
  - Feels intimidated by the complex digital listing processes on traditional sites.
- **Technical Literacy:** Medium. Uses WhatsApp and Instagram heavily but is easily overwhelmed by complex forms.
- **HomeLink Solution Match:** The simple, guided listing process and the exclusivity of the *HomeLink Verified Badge* protect her property's market value.

## 9. Implementation
- Designers must refer to "Arya" or "Maya" during design critiques (e.g., "Would Arya understand this filter?").
